## Suggested study route

| Session | Topics | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–2 | Explain cloud, resources, and availability |
| 2 | 3–4 | Choose compute and storage |
| 3 | 5–7 | Reason about networking, identity, and rules |
| 4 | 8–9 | Investigate issues and spending |
| 5 | 10–12 | Design and deliver changes |
| 6 | 13–14 | Practise, clean up, and review |

You do not need to memorise every service. After each chapter, explain why you would choose an option and which responsibilities would remain yours.

## Scope

An expanded conceptual guide; it does not fully cover AZ-900, AZ-104, AZ-305, or AZ-400. For certification, check current Microsoft Learn objectives. Service options, licences, and regions may change.
